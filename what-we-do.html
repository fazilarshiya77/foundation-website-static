Our Programmes

🐾 Program 1: Feeding of Stray Animals

Every single day, we feed over 60 street dogs across Eastern part of Bangalore, ensuring they don't go to sleep hungry.

Our feeding program provides not just nutrition, but also builds trust and familiarity with the animals.

For many of these dogs, this is their only reliable source of food, love, and human connection.

🐾 Program 2: Sterilisation of Stray Animals

We actively conduct sterilisation drives to control the stray population humanely. Sterilisation helps prevent unwanted litters and reduces territorial aggression and fights.

This effort ensures a safer, healthier life for both animals and the communities they live in.

🐾 Program 3: Rescue and Rehabilitation of Injured Animals

We respond to emergencies by rescuing street animals that are injured, sick, or in distress.

Once they receive medical treatment and recover, we try to find them loving permanent homes/return the fully recovered dogs to their own territory or in case of special need dogs, we do our best to rehabilitate them with reliable and good shelters.

Our goal is not just to heal their wounds but to offer them a second chance at life.

🐾 Program 4: Medical Well-being

From de-worming and anti-tick treatments to surgeries and chronic care, we manage it all.

We monitor the dogs closely and treat them, particularly for infections and other health issues.

A healthy dog is a happy dog, and our medical care ensures they thrive on the streets.
