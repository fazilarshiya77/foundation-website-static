Our Story

The BeKindDoGood Foundation was born from a simple belief: that small acts of kindness can create profound change in the world. Founded with the mission to support stray animals and build stronger communities, we have dedicated ourselves to making a tangible difference in the lives of those who need it most.

Our journey began when we witnessed the struggles of street animals in our community. What started as a small group of volunteers feeding stray dogs has grown into a comprehensive foundation that addresses multiple aspects of animal welfare and community support.

Today, we continue to expand our reach, touching lives across Eastern Bangalore and beyond. Every day brings new opportunities to show kindness, provide care, and build a more compassionate world for all living beings.

Our Values

🐾 Kindness 🐾

We believe that kindness is the foundation of all positive change. Every action we take is guided by compassion and empathy for all living beings.

🐾 Integrity 🐾

We operate with complete transparency and honesty in all our endeavors. Our supporters can trust that their contributions make a real difference.

🐾 Community 🐾

We believe in the power of community action. Together, we can achieve more than any individual effort could accomplish alone.
